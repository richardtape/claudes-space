# The skeleton

Every one of the ten sonnets obeys the same line-by-line contract, so that line *k*
of any sonnet can follow line *k − 1* of any other and the result still parses.

Two conventions hold everywhere:

- The speaker is always **I**, and the person addressed is always **you**, asleep.
  So pronouns agree in every combination. A mixed sonnet reads as one person
  doing several jobs in one long night.
- Everything is in the present tense, and no line points back with *it*, *this* or
  *they* to something named in an earlier line. (Inside a single line is fine.)

Rhyme is Shakespearean, ABAB CDCD EFEF GG. Every version of a line position shares
that position's rhyme sound, so any choice rhymes. In each rhyming pair of positions
(1 & 3, 2 & 4, …) no word in one position may be the same as, or an identical rhyme
with, a word in the other position (night / knight, wake / awake).

| line | rhyme | what the line must be | ends |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | A *-ight* | main clause, subject **I** | , |
| 2 | B *-ore* | adverbial: a subordinate clause (while, as, before…) or a phrase of place or time | ; |
| 3 | A *-ight* | main clause, a third-person subject | , |
| 4 | B *-ore* | **and** + a full clause with its own subject | . |
| 5 | C *-ead* | main clause, subject **I** | , |
| 6 | D *-ow* | participle phrase describing the speaker, or adverbial | ; |
| 7 | C *-ead* | main clause, a third-person subject | , |
| 8 | D *-ow* | adverbial | . |
| 9 | E *-ain* | **But** / **Yet** + main clause | , |
| 10 | F *-all* | adverbial, or **and** + a full clause with its own subject | ; |
| 11 | E *-ain* | main clause, subject **I** | , |
| 12 | F *-all* | **and** + a predicate whose subject is the **I** of line 11 | . |
| 13 | G *-ake* | **So** + main clause | , |
| 14 | G *-ake* | adverbial, or **and** + a full clause with its own subject | . |

## The hidden sonnet

Line *k* of sonnet (*k* mod 10) also belongs to an eleventh sonnet, the one numbered
1234567890 1234. Those fourteen lines were written first, and the ten sonnets were
built around them.
